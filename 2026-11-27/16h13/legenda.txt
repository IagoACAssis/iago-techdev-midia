Habit rings that make you want to finish the day. 🔥
Anéis de hábito que dão vontade de fechar o dia. 🔥

TweenAnimationBuilder fills the rings, AnimatedSwitcher pops the check, Dismissible completes with a swipe and a CustomPainter throws the confetti.
O TweenAnimationBuilder enche os anéis, o AnimatedSwitcher troca pro check, o Dismissible conclui no arrasto e um CustomPainter solta o confete.

Swipe or tap: how would you complete a habit? / Arrastar ou tocar: como você concluiria? 👇
